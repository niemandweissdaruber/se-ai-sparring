About the cost estimates

Estimated from token counts and configured rates. The rates are taken from published provider pricing pages and are not confirmed against a real bill — a promotional rate on a public page doesn't prove your account receives it. Your bill may also differ due to caching, tier pricing, or rate changes. Check your provider dashboard for exact figures: console.anthropic.com and platform.openai.com.

Token counts come straight from each API response. Reasoning tokens are billed at the output rate, which is why output counts can run higher than the visible reply suggests. Rates live in the PRICING object in this page's source.

Stopping a reply does not make it free. The provider bills for whatever it already generated. When you press Stop, the exact token counts never arrive, so the figures for that message are worked out here from the text received — shown with ≈ instead of ~, and likely a slight under-estimate.
